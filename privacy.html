Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Short version: Cruise Line Architect does not collect your personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites. The app does send anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, and nothing in them can identify you. Your saved games stay on your device.

Information we collect

No personal information. The app has no user accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, or advertising identifiers, and the app does not track you across other apps or websites. There is no advertising SDK in Cruise Line Architect, so you will never see a tracking permission prompt.

Anonymous usage statistics

To understand how far players get, and whether the game is too hard, too slow, or asks to be paid for too early, the app sends a small number of anonymous events using TelemetryDeck, a privacy-focused analytics service. They contain counts and fixed categories only:

  • that the app was opened, and whether a saved game already existed;
  • that a new cruise line was started, and in which save slot (never its name);
  • the first time each line designs an itinerary, with its cruising region and number of nights (never its name or its ports);
  • the first time each line acquires a ship, with how it was acquired (new, used or chartered) and its size class (never its name);
  • the first time each line completes a cruise, and the first time it signs a supply contract, with that contract's term;
  • that a line went public, and the share of the company it sold (never its ticker);
  • that the upgrade screen was shown, what prompted it, the game day and how many ships you had, and whether it was closed or the game was unlocked, including which unlock option was chosen (never a price);
  • that a purchase was restored.

TelemetryDeck's software also adds standard technical details to each event: the app version, the iOS version, the device model and screen size, your language, region and time zone settings, light or dark mode, accessibility settings such as text size, and the day and hour of the event. It also counts app sessions, how long they last, and on how many days the app is used.

Crash and performance reports

When the app crashes or freezes, iOS gives it a diagnostic report on a later launch. The app sends only a short summary of that report through TelemetryDeck so we can find and fix the problem: a code for the kind of crash, or how long a freeze lasted (under 3 seconds, 3 to 10 seconds, or longer), and the iOS version. It never sends the report itself, the app's code trail, any game content, or anything about you.

What is never sent: your line's name, your ship and route names, your company's ticker, the ports you choose, your fares, your finances, your saved games, or anything else you create in the game.

TelemetryDeck is designed to be anonymous: it does not receive a name, an email address, or an advertising identifier, and it replaces the device's identifier with a one-way hash that cannot be traced back to you and is not shared with anyone. On the App Store this is declared as Usage Data (product interaction), Identifiers (that hashed device identifier) and Diagnostics (crash data and performance data), not linked to your identity and not used for tracking.

Your game data

Saved games are stored locally on your device, in up to three save slots. They are not synced and never reach us, so we cannot read, recover, or delete them. If you have device backups enabled, your saves may be included in your own Apple backup, which is handled entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Game Center

If you are signed into Game Center, the achievements you earn and your leaderboard scores are sent to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center profile, where your friends may see them. This is handled by Apple and covered by Apple's Privacy Policy; we only see the same leaderboards anyone can see.

Purchases

Cruise Line Architect offers an optional one-time purchase, Cruise Line Architect Pro. There are no subscriptions. Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never see or handle your payment details. We use RevenueCat to verify the purchase, which receives an anonymous identifier and the purchase receipt from Apple in order to tell the app whether the game is unlocked. It does not receive personal information from us. On the App Store this is declared as Purchases (purchase history), not linked to your identity and not used for tracking.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information, because we do not collect any. Three third parties are involved: Apple, which processes all payments and provides Game Center and device backups; RevenueCat, which verifies the purchase; and TelemetryDeck, which receives the anonymous usage statistics and crash reports described above.
