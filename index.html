Frequently asked

How do I get started?

Name your line and you begin with $75 million. On the Routes tab, tap Design new itinerary: pick a region, where guests board and leave, the ports of call, the nights and the fare. On the Line tab, tap Acquire ship — new from the yard, used, or chartered. Then tap Assign vessel on the route's card, and watch her sail on the Map.

Is it a subscription?

No. The free version plays the whole game; a one-time purchase unlocks the full game for good. There are no ads.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so make sure you're signed in with the same one you bought the unlock with.

Why did my ship sail half-empty?

Cabins sell through the weeks before each departure, so a ship assigned the day before she sails has had no time to sell. Give a new route time, run a launch campaign, or sail in the January–March wave season, when bookings come fastest.

Why can't this ship call at that port?

Every port has a real berth length and channel depth, and a ship that doesn't fit is refused. River longships sail only rivers, and Antarctica takes only ice-class expedition ships. Tap the port on the Map to see its limits.

My ship ran out of fresh food. What happened?

Fresh food lasts about nine days at sea; frozen and dry stores last much longer. On long voyages, open the route's Supply Plan to see where she loads, and fix any gap it flags. You don't need a vendor contract — without one, ships buy at the pier at spot prices.

Why can't my ship sail?

Every hull needs a drydock at least every five years. Miss the class survey and she finishes her cruise, then stays in port until she is drydocked. The game warns you 30 days ahead in the activity feed.

Does the game run while I'm away?

No. Time only moves while the game is open, and it waits while a decision card is on screen. You can speed it up to 25×.

I lost a saved game.

Saves live on your device, in up to three slots; they are not synced, and we never receive your game data, so we can't recover a deleted save. If a slot says Saved game (unreadable), the game is protecting the file rather than overwriting it — don't delete it, and write to us first.
